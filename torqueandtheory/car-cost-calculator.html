Your result

Estimated economic cost

Annualised cost based on your assumptions. Economic cost includes depreciation and financing costs, but not the return of your own capital. Optional hassle and flexibility values make explicit costs that ordinary car calculators often ignore.

What the calculation counts

A useful reality check

  • Depreciation is based on the value you enter today and the value you expect later.
  • Finance uses an amortising loan and accounts for the remaining balance if you sell early.
  • Tax effects, insurance excesses, opportunity cost of invested cash and unusual repair events are not modelled unless you put them into the inputs.
Change any input and the result updates instantly.
This is an editorial model, not financial advice. Results are only as good as the assumptions you enter. A lower economic cost does not necessarily mean a better car, and a higher cost does not necessarily mean a worse decision.
